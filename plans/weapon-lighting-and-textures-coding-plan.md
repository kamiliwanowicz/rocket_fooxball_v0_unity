## Objective

Make the first-person rocket launcher and shotgun remain readable when the player turns, while moving their material response toward the attached Quake 2 hi-res reference: warm worn khaki/brass metal, black structure, restrained red accents, broad highlights, and scratches that stay attached to the surface. Complete the work through a fast, bake-free author/capture/reject loop, then one production lighting preparation and separate persisted validation after a fresh `sol_high` visual verifier accepts both weapons.

## Scope

- in: launcher and shotgun PBR atlas synthesis; the eight live launcher/shotgun materials; FPS viewmodel layer, light, and lifecycle; the single arena-global sun and ambient/reflection interaction affecting weapons; removal of arena point/spot fill lights; Low additional-light support; generated prefab/scene/settings/baked outputs; deterministic weapon capture and independent visual review
- out: arena textures, materials, meshes, tiling, sky art, fog, post-processing look, weapon geometry, UVs, material slot order, transforms, animation, recoil, firing/gameplay, HUD, world-shotgun composition, custom shaders, hand editing in Unity, routine Development bakes
